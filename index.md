# Privacy Policy for MeowCook

**Effective Date:** 23.11.2026  
**Last Updated:** 23.11.2026  

**MeowCook** ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information about you is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile application, **MeowCook** (the "App").

---

## 1. Summary of Our Privacy Commitment

- **Local First:** Your recipes, cooking logs, meal plans, ingredients, categories, and custom media are stored locally on your device.
- **No User Registration:** We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, address, or phone number.
- **Third-Party Services:** We use trusted third-party services (Google AdMob, Google Firebase, and Google Play Billing) to display advertisements, manage optional subscriptions, and gather anonymized diagnostics to improve app stability.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
The App stores the following data locally on your device using an embedded database:

- **Recipe & Planning Content:** Recipe titles, descriptions, step-by-step instructions, ingredients, cooking times, difficulty levels, nutrition info, personal notes, and meal plans.
- **Media Files:** Photos or videos you capture or attach to your recipes and cooking steps.
- **App Preferences:** Saved settings (e.g., theme preferences, timer sound/vibration settings, and notification preferences).
- **Import/Export Data:** Backup or recipe files you export or import using the `.meowcook` file format.

*This data remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.*

---

### B. Information Processed by Third-Party Services
When you use the App, certain information is automatically collected or processed by third-party SDKs to support advertisements, subscriptions, crash reporting, and analytics:

1. **Google AdMob (Advertising)**
   - **Data Collected:** Device Advertising ID, coarse location data (derived from IP address), app interaction data, and device information.
   - **Purpose:** To serve contextual and personalized advertisements within the free version of the App.
   - **Privacy Policy:** [Google Advertising Policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

2. **Google Firebase Analytics**
   - **Data Collected:** Anonymized usage statistics (e.g., screens viewed, features used, cooking sessions completed), device hardware type, OS version, language, and session durations.
   - **Purpose:** To understand user engagement and improve feature usability.
   - **Privacy Policy:** [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

3. **Google Firebase Crashlytics**
   - **Data Collected:** Crash logs, stack traces, installation UUIDs, system state information, and device hardware specifications at the time of a crash.
   - **Purpose:** To detect bugs, fix stability issues, and optimize performance.
   - **Privacy Policy:** [Firebase Privacy & Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

4. **Google Play Billing (In-App Subscriptions)**
   - **Data Collected:** Purchase tokens, subscription plan status, and anonymized transaction identifiers processed via Google Play.
   - **Purpose:** To verify active subscriptions and unlock premium features. We do not receive or store your credit card or payment details.
   - **Privacy Policy:** [Google Payments Privacy Notice](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice)

---

## 3. Device Permissions

To provide specific cooking and recipe management features, MeowCook requests the following permissions only when needed:

- **Camera (`CAMERA`) & Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** Required only if you choose to capture recipe photos or step videos directly within the App. Selecting existing photos or videos uses Android's system media picker without granting full storage access.
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used to display cooking timer alerts and controls when steps are running or complete.
- **Exact Alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `USE_EXACT_ALARM`):** Ensures cooking step timers trigger accurately even when the App is in the background or the screen is locked.
- **Vibration & Wake Lock (`VIBRATE` / `WAKE_LOCK`):** Used to trigger tactile timer alerts and the optional "keep screen awake" mode while cooking.
- **Internet & Network State (`INTERNET` / `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`):** Required for fetching ad content, loading remote recipe preview assets, verifying subscriptions, sending anonymous crash diagnostics, and opening external links.

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is processed only by the third-party providers listed in Section 2B (Google AdMob, Google Firebase, and Google Play) strictly for operational, advertising, billing, and analytical purposes.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

---

## 5. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

- **Manage or Delete Local Data:** You have full control over your local content. Deleting a recipe or clearing App storage in Android Settings permanently removes that data from your device. Uninstalling the App deletes all local databases and preferences.
- **Opt Out of Personalized Ads:** You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings (**Settings > Google > Ads** or **Settings > Privacy > Ads**), or through the in-app consent prompt where applicable.
- **EEA, UK, and US Regional Rights:** Depending on your location (such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California), you may have rights under GDPR or local privacy laws to access, restrict, or request deletion of data processed by third-party analytics/ad providers. Because MeowCook does not maintain user accounts on external servers, we cannot identify individual users directly, but you can exercise these controls through your device settings or contact us for assistance.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- **Local Data:** Retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
- **Analytics & Diagnostic Data:** Retained by Google Firebase according to their standard data retention schedules (typically up to 14 months for Analytics and 90 days for Crashlytics logs).

## 7. Children's Privacy

MeowCook is a general-audience cooking application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, such as 16 in the EEA/UK). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.
